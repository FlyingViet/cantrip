Connect to your Mac

Cantrip Gateway shows Cantrip Home, your project tabs, tasks and artifacts from Cantrip on your Mac. Pair only with a Mac you control: everything you send runs there.

Pairing link

On the Mac, run cantrip pair --direct and paste the link it prints, or fill in the fields below.

Mac

The Mac's address and the encrypted port shown in Cantrip's Remote control settings on the Mac. Works on the same network, or over Tailscale with the Mac's Tailscale address.

Used when the direct address can't be reached, for example away from home.
