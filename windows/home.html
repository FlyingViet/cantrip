Pair with your Mac

Cantrip Home runs on your Mac. Pair this PC to see Home's conversation, its background runs and tasks here, with their cards.

On the Mac, run cantrip pair and paste the cantripagent://pair link it prints. Cantrip Gateway's pairing links work too.

The token is stored encrypted for this Windows account and is only sent to your Mac over its encrypted home-network connection or its Tailscale HTTPS address. Nothing is shared through a public link.
